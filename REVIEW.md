# REVIEW.md

List every defect you found in the starter `app/main.py` (helpers and endpoints). For each one:

| # | Where (function / line) | What is wrong | How you'd notice it (test, input, or symptom) | How you fixed it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `health()` | Returned HTTP 200 unconditionally without pinging MongoDB | Database could be offline or unreachable while `/health` reported healthy | Added `db.command("ping")` inside a `try/except` block, raising HTTP 503 (`database unavailable`) on failure |
| 2 | `EpochMillisType` / timestamp parsing | Allowed floats, strings, booleans, and seconds-range integers (< 100,000,000,000) | `punched_at=1783310280000.0` or `punched_at=True` was accepted; seconds passed as ms | Enforced strict `type(v) is int and not isinstance(v, bool)` and range `100_000_000_000 <= v <= 4_102_444_800_000`, rejecting with 422 |
| 3 | `half_up()` / rounding | Built-in `round()` uses banker's rounding (round-to-even), diverging on `.5` boundaries | `round(9.125, 2)` produced `9.12` instead of `9.13`; `round(2.5, 0)` gave `2.0` instead of `3.0` | Implemented `half_up()` using `Decimal.quantize(ROUND_HALF_UP)` across all business calculations (R4, R8) |
| 4 | `EmployeeIn` validation | Allowed `shift_start == shift_end` and loose `emp_code` format | Created employees with 0-minute shifts or invalid codes like `EMP123` or lowercase `emp0001` | Added model validator raising 422 when `shift_start == shift_end` and regex `^EMP\d{4,6}$` |
| 5 | `compute_attendance_date()` (R1) | Did not handle overnight shift punch-ins occurring before `shift_end` next morning | Punch-in at 05:45 IST on July 7 for shift 22:00-06:00 was assigned to July 7 instead of July 6 | Checked overnight condition (`shift_end <= shift_start` and IST time `< shift_end`), attributing record to previous calendar date |
| 6 | `compute_late_minutes()` (R2) | Missing 10-minute grace window and floor minute measurement | Arrival at 09:40:01 IST was either not penalized or calculated as 1 minute instead of 10 | Implemented R2 rule: `diff <= 10` returns 0; `diff > 10` returns full elapsed integer minutes from `shift_start` |
| 7 | `compute_overtime_minutes()` (R3) | Counted overtime without enforcing the 30-minute qualification threshold | 5 or 29 minutes past shift end counted as overtime | Implemented R3 rule: overtime is 0 if `diff < 30`; otherwise `int(diff)` in whole floor minutes |
| 8 | `compute_work_hours()` (R4) | Did not truncate sub-seconds to whole seconds before computing duration | Microseconds caused fractional second drift and rounding errors | Truncated punch instants to whole seconds (`ms // 1000 * 1000`) before duration calculation and applied `half_up(..., 2)` |
| 9 | `compute_half_day()` (R5) | Evaluated boundary with `<=` or flagged open records | Exactly 4.50 hours incorrectly set `half_day = True` | Corrected boundary to strictly `work_hours < 4.50`, returning `False` when `work_hours is None` or `>= 4.50` |
| 10 | `POST /attendance/punch-in` | Check-then-insert pattern was vulnerable to race conditions | Concurrent identical punch-in requests both inserted records and returned 201 | Created unique compound index `IX4_emp_code_date_unique`; handled `DuplicateKeyError` to return HTTP 409 |
| 11 | `POST /attendance/punch-out` | Non-atomic punch-out and missing 24-hour timestamp validation | Concurrent punch-outs both succeeded; punch-outs accepted instants before punch-in or days later | Used atomic `find_one_and_update` on open record (`punch_out: None`); validated `punch_in < punched_at <= punch_in + 24h` with 422 |
| 12 | `PATCH /attendance/{emp_code}/{date}` | Unprotected read-modify-write lost concurrent regularization history | Two simultaneous regularizations overwrote each other's audit history | Added Compare-And-Swap (CAS) matching `history` array length (`{"$size": len(history)}`), returning 409 on race |
| 13 | `GET /attendance` | Mixed sort `date DESC, emp_code ASC` triggered in-memory sorts on large collections | Slow queries and `COLLSCAN` on large datasets | Created compound index `IX5_date_desc_emp_code_asc` matching exact sort direction |
| 14 | Analytics Headcount (R9) | Aggregations queried `attendance_logs` first, dropping zero-log employees | Active employees with no punches were excluded from department headcount | Pipelined from `employees` matching `joined_on <= cutoff`, then `$lookup` into `attendance_logs` |
| 15 | Department Trend (R7, R9) | Omitted dates with zero attendance records or weekend days | Missing calendar dates in date range; gaps in trend timeline | Used MongoDB `$densify` on daily dates to guarantee every single calendar day is present |
| 16 | Serializers & Responses | Leaked internal `_id` and added non-contract `id` field | Response schemas failed openapi validation against contract models | Stripped `_id` across all endpoints and adhered strictly to documented response models |

### Non-Defects Considered and Retained
- **Plain string dates and shift times:** Storing `date`, `joined_on`, `shift_start`, and `shift_end` as strings (`"YYYY-MM-DD"`, `"HH:MM"`) rather than BSON datetimes is intentional per `DATA_MODEL.md §1`. Calendar dates and shift hours are timezone-independent business definitions; forcing UTC datetimes onto them introduces timezone shift bugs.
- **No API for creating `ABSENT` / `LEAVE` records:** The punch endpoints only support `PRESENT`, `WFH`, and `ON_DUTY`. `ABSENT` and `LEAVE` represent seeded administrative records or manual regularizations, not physical punches.
